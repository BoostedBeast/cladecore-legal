Cladecore — Account & data deletion

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data related to Cladecore, and what the developer can (and cannot) delete.

Cladecore does not run its own email/password accounts or a separate player account database. Optional sign-in uses Google Play Games (your Google account). Ads use Google AdMob.

How to request deletion from us

Open a request using either:

  1. Discord — use the invite link in the game under Archive → Social, and message about “Cladecore data deletion,” or
  2. GitHub — open an issue on the public cladecore-legal repository with the title Account / data deletion request.

Please say what you want removed (for example: support messages, help with Cladecore cloud save, or confirmation that local data is on you to clear). You do not need to send Google Play order numbers or account passwords — we cannot look up or wipe Google accounts from an admin panel.

We aim to respond within 30 days.

What we can delete or help remove

Data Who controls it What we can do
Local save on your device You / your device We cannot reach your phone. You delete it by clearing Cladecore app storage or uninstalling the app.
Play Games cloud save snapshot (if you signed in) Google Play Games + the game’s snapshot for Cladecore We do not have an admin panel to wipe arbitrary players’ Google accounts. If you contact us, we will guide you and, where the Play Games APIs and your cooperation allow (e.g. while signed in), help remove Cladecore cloud save data associated with your play.
Support / request records (Discord messages, GitHub issues you opened) Us / those platforms On request, we can delete or anonymize messages and tickets we control that identify your deletion request, where the platform allows.
AdMob / advertising identifiers Google and ad partners We do not operate ad servers. Manage ad personalization and related data via Google account / device ad settings and Google’s privacy tools.
Your Google / Play Games account itself Google We cannot delete your Google account. Use Google’s account controls.

Where else to delete or manage data

On your device

  • Settings → Apps → Cladecore → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall Cladecore. This removes local progress on that device.

Google Play Games / Google account

  • Manage Play Games profile and game activity in the Play Games app / Google account settings.
  • Review Google’s help for deleting or managing Play Games data and activity (search Google Help for “delete Play Games data” / manage activity).
  • Your Google account deletion or data tools: Google Account and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

  • Device settings for ad ID / ads personalization (varies by Android version).
  • Google ad settings where applicable.

What this request does not do

  • It does not delete your entire Google account.
  • It does not erase data held only by Google, carriers, or other apps we do not control.
  • It does not refund Play purchases by itself (use Play’s refund / purchase tools where eligible).
