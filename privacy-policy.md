# Cladecore Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

Cladecore (“the game”, “we”) is an idle / incremental mobile game. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

## Who we are

Cladecore is published by BoostedBeast. Contact: Discord invite linked in the game’s Archive → Social section, or via the GitHub repository for this project.

## Data we collect and why

### Gameplay progress (on your device and optional cloud)

- Local save data on your device (progress, currencies, settings).
- If you sign in with **Google Play Games**, we may store a cloud save snapshot so you can restore progress on another device. This uses Google Play Games Services; we do not operate a separate player account database.

### Advertising (AdMob)

- The game uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** for optional rewarded ads (for example chest opens, tickets, Lab keys).
- Google and its partners may collect advertising identifiers and device information to serve and measure ads, subject to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and AdMob policies.
- Where required (EEA/UK/Switzerland), a consent form (UMP) may be shown before personalized ads.

### Analytics / diagnostics

- Basic crash or funnel logs may be collected to improve stability. We do not sell personal information.

### Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — ads, Play Games sign-in, cloud save, and network time checks for offline progress.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not require an email/password account for core play (Play Games sign-in is optional).

## Your choices

- You can decline rewarded ads and still play; some optional rewards may use Ad Skip Tokens or gems instead.
- You can clear app data / uninstall to remove local saves.
- You can manage Play Games and ad personalization in your device / Google account settings.

## Children

Cladecore is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the game if you are under the applicable age in your region without a parent/guardian.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

Questions: use the in-game Discord link or open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository.
